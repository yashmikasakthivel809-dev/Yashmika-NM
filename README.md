LegalEase: AI-Powered Legal Document Generator
     Project Overview
LegalEase is an AI-powered legal document generator designed to help users create legal documents easily and efficiently.
Objective:
The main objective of LegalEase is to simplify the process of generating commonly required legal documents through an easy-to-use system.

 Generate legal documents
